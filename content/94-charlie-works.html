The question

Seven years after the scene of Chapter 93, an attempt to build it. The note’s own header states the occasion with unusual directness: the blog post was seven years ago, now we can build it, publish in time to discuss at a conference in January 2025.

It is the only chapter in the collection reporting an experiment, and the finding is about data rather than about the model.

94.1 The setup

A fictional person was given a simulated personal store containing data of the kind a real person accumulates: banking, fitness, and the rest. A large language model was connected to it. And the interface carried one control:

The Charlie app has a button which allows Bob to give or not give consent. Charlie Works, 2025

One button, two answers to the same question, which makes this the only controlled comparison in the book.

94.2 The two answers

Berners-Lee’s assessment sets it against what people had already found remarkable:

Even though a couple of years ago people were amazed at how useful ChatGPT or followers like Claude, Pi, and so on, this is up anther level. This is what i want. Charlie Works, 2025

94.3 The disclaimers, which are the honest part

The note does not overstate what was shown. The person is fictional and the data simulated. And the obstacle to a real version is named exactly:

Extracting data from the world of APIs into a pod is a lot of work. Charlie Works, 2025

That sentence is the state of the whole programme. The store works, the model works, the combination works, and getting a real person’s real data out of the places that hold it is the remaining labour. Chapter 93 said the obstacle was never technical, and this confirms it from the other side: the technical part succeeded first.

Berners-Lee adds a note of optimism that is itself circular in an interesting way: it is work an assistant can help with, since it can build the importers.

94.4 Four findings

The conclusions are stated as system design observations, and the last two are the ones that matter for the future of this book’s subject.

FindingWhat it means
The data infrastructure is as always criticalThe model is not the scarce part
A store of structured data is powerful in driving the modelVolume IV’s work pays off here
The model both fills the store and reads from itIt populates the graph and then uses it as context
The graph becomes the medium of communication between modelsSystems talk to each other through the data, not directly

The third breaks a circularity that had seemed fatal. Chapter 72 observed that structured data about private life does not exist to be learned from. Here the model is used to create it, from whatever form it is in, and then to read it. The expensive step becomes something the machine does.

The fourth is the more consequential claim. If systems communicate through a shared graph rather than through each other, then everything in Volumes IV to VI applies: identifiers that mean the same thing to everyone, vocabularies mixed from several communities, statements that can be checked, and a store whose owner decides who reads it.

94.5 What the pair of chapters demonstrates

Chapter 93 imagined a capability and an arrangement. The capability arrived from somewhere else entirely and the arrangement did not. This chapter puts the two together in a laboratory and shows the difference is large.

What it does not show is anyone getting there. Between the demonstration and a person using it lies the work of extracting a life from the services that hold it, and those services have no reason to help.

What happened since

  • Connecting assistants to personal data became ordinary. Every large provider wired its assistant to the documents and mail it already held, which is the concentration Chapter 72 warned of.
  • Standard connectors emerged. Protocols for giving models access to tools and data sources were published and adopted rapidly from late 2024, making the plumbing this note calls a lot of work into a common component.
  • The graph-as-medium claim is being tested. Whether models coordinate through shared structured data or through each other directly is now an open architectural question, and it is the one section 94.4 raises.
  • Nobody has a real pod full of their life. The extraction problem remains the binding constraint, as this note says.
