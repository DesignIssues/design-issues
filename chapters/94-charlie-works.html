Chapter 94

Charlie Works

2025

Source Charlie Works, a Design Issues note by Tim Berners-Lee, 2025-01-13. read the original

The short version

Engineers asked the same assistant the same question twice, with one switch deciding whether it could see a (fake) person’s personal data. With the data it gave a far better answer. The hard part left is getting real data out of the services that hold it today.

The question

Seven years after the scene of Chapter 93, an attempt to build it. The note, dated 13 January 2025, states its occasion directly in its header: the blog post was seven years ago, now we can build it, publish in time to discuss at Davos in 2025.

Unusually for this collection, it reports an experiment, and the finding is about data rather than about the model.

94.1 The setup

Chapter 93 imagined Charlie, an assistant that works for its user, Bob, and can see everything about him. Late in 2024, engineers at Inrupt built Charlie. They invented Bob and filled a Solid pod for him (Chapter 54: a personal data store that its owner controls) with simulated data from his bank, from Strava, the running app, and so on. The pod holds this as linked, structured statements, a graph, rather than as loose files. Then they connected Claude, Anthropic’s large language model, and let Bob pass his pod to it.

A large language model is the kind of program behind ChatGPT: it answers in prose from what it was trained on, plus whatever it is given to read. The pod was the extra reading. The interface carried one control:

The Charlie app has a button which allows Bob to give or not give consent. Charlie Works, 2025

94.3 The disclaimers

The note states its limits. Bob is fictional and his pod simulated. And the obstacle to a real version is named exactly:

Extracting data from the world of APIs into a pod is a lot of work. Charlie Works, 2025

Chapter 93 named three routes to Bob’s data (anger, law, architecture). This shows the architecture working; filling it is the remaining work.

Berners-Lee adds that this is work AI can help with, because AI can build the apps that import the data.

94.4 Four findings

The conclusions are stated as system design observations, and the last two matter most for this book’s subject.

FindingWhat it means
The data infrastructure is as always criticalThe model is not the scarce part
A pod of semantic web data is extremely powerful in driving the modelThe structured-data work of Volume IV pays off here
The model both fills the pod and reads from itIt populates the graph and then uses it as context
The graph becomes the medium of communication between modelsSystems talk to each other through the data, not directly

The third suggests a way round Chapter 72’s problem, that structured data about private life does not exist to learn from: the model can help create that data, and then read it.

The fourth is the more consequential claim. If systems communicate through a shared graph rather than through each other, then, the book argues, everything in Volumes IV to VI applies: identifiers that mean the same thing to everyone, vocabularies mixed from several communities, statements that can be checked, and a pod whose owner decides who reads it.

94.5 What the pair of chapters demonstrates

Chapter 93 imagined a capability and an arrangement. The capability, fluent language models, came from outside the Solid work; the arrangement, data the user controls, did not arrive. This chapter puts the two together in a laboratory and shows the difference is large.

Getting real data there is another matter: in this book’s view, the services that hold it have little reason to help.

What happened since

  • Connecting assistants to personal data became ordinary. Microsoft 365 Copilot, generally available from November 2023, wired an assistant to the mail and documents Microsoft already held, which is the concentration Chapter 72 warned of.
  • Standard connectors emerged. Anthropic published the Model Context Protocol in November 2024, an open standard for giving models access to tools and data sources. OpenAI adopted it in March 2025, making part of the plumbing this note calls a lot of work into a common component.
  • Law reached device data. The EU Data Act, applying from 12 September 2025, lets users obtain the data their connected devices generate and pass it to a third party.

Connections
